The A/B of 2026-11-25-ab runs again, on a GitHub-hosted runner (ubuntu-24.04),
because the Mac's 1 min load never allowed the declared gate and the gate-8 run
(2026-11-26-ab-gate8) cannot clear the new build. The gate is the declared one:
before each trial the 1 min load average (/proc/loadavg) must have stayed at
most 4.0 (MAX_LOAD) for 30 s in a row (LOAD_QUIET), polled every 5 s, within
1800 s (MAXWAIT); a load that cannot be read is not quiet. The bar is not
lowered on the runner.

The design is unchanged from 2026-11-25-ab: four blocks on one runner, old
(821870c), new (840bb74), old, new, each one baseline trial (FreeSWITCH, room
1004) and one candidate trial (chappe_mix, room 1003) per clip and direction
(the 8 VCTK clips, g722-to-opus and opus-to-g722), and the margins block
declared at 2026-11-25T22:38:12Z unchanged (sha256
268e6bca4d9306a2a6939ede8017565bcfdf2331786c3c5d9c3840366039a997). As in the
gate-8 run, a refused trial, the campaign's first included, is tried again up
to four more times (block.sh's RETRIES), a trial still refused stops the run,
and the rtpengine-loss sessions stay. No trial and no retry starts after the
deadline, 285 min after the job's start (the job is cut at 350 min): the run
then stops at a trial boundary.

What differs from the Mac runs: taq does not run on the runner. trial.sh makes
taq live's two calls (same order, durations, ports and pjsua options) through
chappe's hd.sh, with chappe's lab pjsua image (pjproject 2.15.1 with Opus and
G.722, without taq's config_site.h, whose L16 at 48 kHz, IPv6 and call count
these trials do not use), built once before the first trial; taq live-import
finishes the trials on the Mac, where they are scored. The host (a hosted VM,
described in each trial.json) and the pjsua build differ from the Mac's, so
absolute scores do not compare with the Mac runs: the question is only old
against new on one runner. The 1 min, 5 min and 15 min load and the CPU steal
are sampled every 10 s into hostload.log.

A run stopped by a refusal or by the deadline is recorded as incomplete, with
what ran, and no score is looked at before the maintainer decides what follows.
The smoke run (a branch ending in -smoke: one block of the new build on clip
p225, both directions) checks the pipeline only and is not part of the A/B.
